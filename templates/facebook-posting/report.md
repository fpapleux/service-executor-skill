# Report: Facebook outcome

Apply the generic customer communication duty at acceptance/rejection decisions, intervention blockers, task failure and completion. Use the explicitly configured authorized communication route/procedure from local tool configuration; this public template prescribes no channel, provider or sender identity.

Report request ID, resolved Page identity, actual publication/scheduling/rejection/blocked/failed/unknown outcome, confirmed effects and evidence, native post reference when readily available, reason and required intervention/next action. Retain the report content and its incident/attempt identity separately from task execution.

Invoke failure/intervention reporting immediately when those conditions are observed, including past-schedule exception, disabled upload permission, unsupported future scheduling and customer login challenges. An unavailable configured communication service leaves pending communication and surfaces the actual gap through any available authorized requester route. Do not invent a missing-credential diagnosis or substitute a route/identity.

Use the configured tool's current documented send/report operation and observe its native acknowledgement. A customer portal with request-specific receipt acknowledgement is a valid route. Record submission/receipt evidence without claiming recipient delivery or reading. Reconcile an unknown submission before any resend; preserve a confirmed incident report without duplication.

A temporary communication outage does not block otherwise accepted, authorized, independently executable posting. A completed post remains completed with reporting pending. Later sessions recover only unfinished reporting and never replay confirmed posting. Preserve rejection/failure truthfully even after its report has been acknowledged.
